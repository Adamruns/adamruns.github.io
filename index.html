At MedShift, I built the Buy Side funding workflow and its QuickBooks Online integration. That means owning the Django APIs, the Vue interfaces, and the reconciliation that lets finance trust the numbers.

One lending-pricing bug meant reconstructing payment schedules and matching the recorded payments to the cent before fixing the calculation. That’s the work I like: follow the evidence until the behavior makes sense.

I also write the agent instructions, pre-PR checks, and AI review workflows our team uses. My M.S. at Clemson focused on AI and machine learning; I finished with a 4.0 while working full-time.
